# Botium Toys Compliance Assessment

Reviewed the Botium Toys compliance checklist as part of the Google Cybersecurity Certificate. Learned how to assess security practices under PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC 1/SOC 2 by evaluating access controls, data protection, data integrity, and data availability.

The assessment focused on comparing the company's current security practices against compliance requirements and identifying areas that may need improvement based on the risk assessment report.
